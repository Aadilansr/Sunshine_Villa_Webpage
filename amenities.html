All yours

A resort full of good ideas.

Thoughtfully designed spaces and services that make every day feel a little effortless.

Infinity pool

A 25-metre pool that seems to spill straight into the ocean, with shaded cabanas and service at your fingertips.

Private beach

300 metres of soft sand with dedicated loungers, parasols and a beach team ready with fresh coconut water.

Spa & wellness

Six treatment rooms, a steam cave and a menu of island-inspired rituals using local oils and flowers.

An air-conditioned gym with ocean views, plus sunrise yoga and sunrise runs along the shore.

Handwoven baskets, local spices, swimwear and thoughtful souvenirs — all sourced from nearby makers.

Concierge service

From restaurant bookings to last-minute requests, our team is available around the clock, every day.

Fast Wi-Fi

Reliable, complimentary Wi-Fi across the entire property — even on the beach, if you really must.

Perfect location

A 20-minute drive from the airport, with easy access to Stone Town, the reef and the island's best markets.
