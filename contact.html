Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is by 11:00. If you arrive early, we will happily look after your bags and get you settled by the pool.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A full breakfast for two is included in every room rate, served daily from 06:30 to 11:00 at Mango & Tide or on your terrace.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Within 48 hours, the first night is charged. We always recommend travel insurance.

Do you arrange airport transfers?

We do — a private return transfer from Abeid Amani Karume Airport is included for every booking. Just send us your flight details a few days before you arrive.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Our kitchen works with vegan, gluten-free, halal and allergy-friendly menus. Let us know in your booking note and we will take care of the rest.

Is the resort family friendly?

Very. We have family suites, a shallow pool area, childcare on request and a team that loves little guests. Cribs and extra beds are available at no charge.
