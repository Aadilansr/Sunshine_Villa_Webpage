From the kitchen

"If it isn't fresh today, it isn't on the menu."

Chef Amina leads our kitchen with a simple philosophy: let the ingredients speak, add a little island soul, and never rush a good meal.

Her team works with nearby fishers and farmers every morning, so what lands on your plate has usually been swimming or growing a few hours earlier.
